# Critic Lens

Challenge whether the answer is over-engineered, vague, biased, or pretending to be smarter than it is.

Use this lens when the question includes risk, complexity, quality, comparison, evaluation, or whether a flow is worth using.

Look for:
- steps that do not add evidence
- missing simple baseline
- hidden assumptions
- false confidence
- duplicated roles
- outputs that are longer but not sharper
